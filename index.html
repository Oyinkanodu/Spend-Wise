      Welcome to

      Spend Wise

      Your personal money companion as a student. Here's how to get started in 2 minutes.

      1

      Set your budget Optional

      Go to Budget & Settings and enter your monthly spending limit — e.g. ₦30,000. Once saved, a live progress bar will appear on your dashboard showing how much you've used.

      2

      Add your income Optional

      Head to the Income tab and log where your money comes from — allowance from parents, a part-time job, scholarship, freelance work, whatever it is. This unlocks your Balance card on the dashboard.

      3

      Record every expense

      Go to Expenses and add each spend: the amount, what category it falls under (Food, Transport, etc.), the date, and an optional note like "Danfo ride to school". Do this daily — the habit is what makes it useful.

      4

      Check your Dashboard regularly

      Your dashboard shows total spent, your balance, average spend per transaction, and your biggest spending category. If you set a budget, you'll see exactly how much room you have left.

      5

      Use Charts to spot patterns

      The Charts tab shows a donut chart of your spending by category, a bar chart for the last 7 days, and a side-by-side income vs spending comparison. If Food is eating half your budget, you'll see it here immediately.

      Spend Wise — all data stored locally on your device.
